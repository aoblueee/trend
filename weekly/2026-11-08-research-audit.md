## Scope and method

- Primary observation window: 2026-10-26–2026-11-08. Older observations are labelled where used.
- General radar excludes songs, artists, and music-derived dance or meme items. Music is evaluated only in Music Radar.
- November cards compare against the final October score. Moves compare this publication with 2026-11-01.
- MAIN is not padded: two non-music items passed the combined gate. A high rank alone was not treated as growth.
- Search trend time series could not be obtained under a comparable public condition. Search momentum is unconfirmed and unused.
