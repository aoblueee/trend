# Trend Radar research audit — 2026-11-01

## Scope and method

- Primary observation window: 2026-10-19–2026-11-01; older observations are identified where used.
- General radar excludes songs, artists, and music-derived dance or meme items. Music is evaluated only in Music Radar.
- Monthly comparison uses the final October score, while Moves describes this publication’s lifecycle decision.
- MAIN is not padded: three non-music items passed the combined gate for freshness, momentum, evidence strength, and source diversity.
- Search trend time series could not be obtained under a comparable public condition. Search momentum is marked unconfirmed and is not used as evidence.
